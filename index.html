Fair Play

A household chore-and-responsibility tracker for two people.

Fair Play is a desktop app that one household uses to keep track of who holds each responsibility card in the Fair Play system for sharing household work. For each card, the household records who owns it, what their agreed minimum standard of care is, and how often the work should happen.

What the app does with Google Calendar

If the user chooses to connect their Google account, the app turns the recurring schedules the household has written into repeating events on Google Calendar so that each partner gets phone alerts for the cards they hold. Specifically, it:

  • creates two calendars in the user's account, one per partner (for example “Fair Play · Alex”);
  • shares those two calendars with the partner's email address that the user types in;
  • adds, updates, moves and deletes only the events that the app itself created, and never touches other events.

Connecting Google is optional. The app is fully usable without it.

Who it is for

This is a private, personal-use app built by and for one household. It is not distributed to the public and does not have a sign-up. This page exists to describe the app and its handling of Google user data.
